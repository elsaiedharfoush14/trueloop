How it is calculated (ASTM D4516 method)

Recovery R = Qp / (Qp + Qc). Feed-concentrate average TDS Cfc = Cf · ln(1/(1−R)) / R. Osmotic pressure π ≈ 0.000689 · TDS · (T+273)/298 bar. Net driving pressure NDP = Pf − ΔP/2 − Pp − (πfc − πp). Temperature correction TCF = exp(U · (1/298 − 1/(273+T))), U = 2640 above 25 °C and 3020 below. Normalized permeate flow NPF = Qp · (NDPbase/NDP) · (TCFbase/TCF). Normalized salt passage NSP = (Cp/Cfc) · (NDP/NDPbase). Normalized ΔP NΔP = ΔP · (Qavg,base/Qavg)^1.5, with Qavg = (Qfeed + Qconc)/2. TDS is estimated from conductivity with the factor you set. Results are for trending; confirm with your membrane supplier's software for warranty claims.
